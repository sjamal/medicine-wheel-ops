# Operational Manual: Decolonizing Engineering Architecture

## 1. System Lineage & Long-Baseline Roots (North)
Infrastructure cannot be safely modified without understanding its ancestral technical footprint. The `LineageRegistry` tracks deep historical narratives, preventing engineering workflows from treating active code bases as unburdened greenfield states.

## 2. Multidimensional Balance vs. Zero-Sum Matrices (East)
Traditional risk management uses linear equations to buy throughput at the cost of team sustainability. This framework rejects extraction by mandating a four-dimensional circular evaluation matrix:
- **System Integrity:** Core operational footprint stability.
- **Operational Burnout:** Toil, page stress, and engineering fatigue thresholds.
- **Resource Overhead:** Accumulation rate of technical complexity.
- **Knowledge Equity:** Democratization and preservation of institutional logic.

## 3. Code Stewardship and Active Tending (South)
Engineering velocity must match architectural cultivation. The pipeline blocks features when codebases are mined for utility without a reciprocal contribution to platform health, enforcing strict equilibrium through quantifiable Tending Ratios.
